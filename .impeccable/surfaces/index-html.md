## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal site that reads like a well-edited one-page document, not a template. Refuses the dark navy hero, mono eyebrows, chip clouds and equal card grid every student portfolio ships.

OWN-WORLD: Light paper-white ground with cool neutral greys, one accent in Queensland maroon (lightened in dark mode). One grotesk family (Schibsted Grotesk) at a small, confident size, tabular numerals for dates and results. Hairline rules, no cards, no chips, no glow.

STORY: In one screen the visitor learns who Vy is, that she ships tested, working software, some of it for Queensland, and that she is available now; then scans finished projects with real results, then experience, then contacts her.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Narrow left-aligned column. Small portrait and name, one sentence of positioning, an availability line with a maroon dot, a row of text links (Resume, Email, GitHub, LinkedIn). The first project, JavaFX Tetris, with its gameplay video, starts inside the first viewport on desktop.
